- **Owner** ✅ (Full access - ONLY owners can manage staff)

## 🎮 **HOW TO USE**

### **Add New Staff Member:**
1. Click **➕ Add Staff** button
2. Fill in employee details:
   - **Full Name** (required)
   - **Email** (required) 
   - **Phone Number**
   - **Role** (Manager/Cashier/Waiter/Kitchen)
   - **Monthly Salary** (MAD)
   - **Hire Date**
3. Click **Add Staff**
4. ✅ New employee added to system

### **Manage Existing Staff:**
1. **View Staff Grid** - See all employees at a glance
2. **Clock In/Out** - Manage employee shifts
3. **Change Status** - Update employee availability
4. **View Details** - See complete employee profile
5. **Performance Tracking** - Monitor productivity

### **Track Performance:**
1. Switch to **📊 Performance** view
2. See staff ranked by performance
3. Monitor key metrics:
   - Orders completed
   - Revenue generated
   - Customer ratings
   - Punctuality scores

### **Manage Schedules:**
1. Switch to **📅 Schedule** view
2. See weekly schedule for all staff
3. Monitor working days and hours
4. Plan shift coverage
